Household E-mail Agent

A private, single-user assistant that helps one person manage their own Gmail inbox.

What it is

Household E-mail Agent is a personal automation tool. It runs on hardware owned and operated by its sole user, on that user's own home network. It is not a commercial product, it is not distributed, and it is not offered to other people. There is no sign-up, no customer base, and no public service behind it.

What it does

Once the owner authorizes it against their own Google Account, the agent connects to that single Gmail mailbox and assists with routine inbox work:

  • Reading and summarizing incoming mail
  • Sorting, labeling, and filing messages
  • Drafting and sending replies on the owner's behalf
  • Surfacing items that need the owner's attention

It acts only on the mailbox of the person who authorized it, and only while that authorization remains in place.

Who it is for

One person: the owner of the account it is connected to. No other users are supported or intended.

Your data

The agent handles the owner's own mail and nothing else. Message content stays on the owner's own machine and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. The Privacy Policy explains in full what is accessed, how it is used, how long it is kept, and how access can be revoked at any time.

Contact

Questions about this application can be sent to the address listed on the Google consent screen for this app.
